How the verdicts are decided
Deliverable
The mail server confirmed the mailbox on a live SMTP probe. Safe to send.
Risky
It resolves, but something weakens it: an accept-all domain, a shared role inbox, a full mailbox or a disposable provider. Sending may bounce or go unread.
Undeliverable
Bad syntax, a domain with no mail server, or an explicit rejection. Remove these from your list.
Unknown
The provider deferred, blocked the probe, or SMTP is disabled. Retry later before deciding.
